Magnetic light baseplate inspiration

RIP_Plo-Koon — Gridfinity Magnet Light Baseplate
https://www.printables.com/model/250550-gridfinity-magnet-light-baseplate
The magnetic light construction independently recreates the open frame,
rounded corner magnet tabs and optional through-holes shown in that design.
Its configurable dimensions are our own; no source CAD or mesh was copied.
This reference is not a claim of identical dimensions or certified physical fit.
