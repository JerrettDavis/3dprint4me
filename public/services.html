SERVICE 03
Repair and tuning

Find the cause instead of stacking another workaround on top.

Consumer printers fail as systems. Motion, extrusion, heat, wiring, firmware, geometry, slicer settings, and material behavior interact. Diagnosis starts with symptoms and evidence, then narrows the fault.

Bench diagnostic from $49Replacement parts and extensive disassembly are quoted separately
MechanicalMotion, belts, bearings, frames, gantries, build surfaces, Z systems, and vibration.
Extrusion and heatHotends, clogs, drive systems, thermistors, heaters, cooling, and flow consistency.
Electrical and firmwareBoards, drivers, sensors, wiring, configuration, Klipper, Marlin, and upgrades.
Calibration and qualityFirst layer, geometry, resonance, pressure/linear advance, flow, temperature, and profiles.

Remote troubleshooting is available where hands-on access is not required. Unsafe mains-voltage conditions may require a licensed electrical professional.

How pricing works

The calculator is a guardrail, not a sales trick.

It combines known cost drivers into a planning range. I then review the actual geometry, risk, fit, parts, schedule, and delivery before confirming anything.

  • PrintingSetup, material, machine time, quality, color changes, finishing, quantity, and delivery.
  • DesignComplexity, source quality, deliverables, iteration, and whether a physical test is included.
  • RepairDiagnostic depth, disassembly, parts, firmware, calibration, and handoff.
Rough estimate means rough estimate.
It is non-binding and usually excludes tax, shipping, replacement components, unusual packaging, third-party licenses, and changes requested after approval. Those items are explicit in the final scope.
