What this work demonstrates

The model is only one part of the solution.

A useful design accounts for the physical object, the printer that will make it, and the person who has to live with it afterward.

  • Measured fitClearance, shrink behavior, insertion, mating geometry, and adjustable parameters.
  • Manufacturable geometryOrientation, support strategy, bridges, overhangs, layer direction, and build-volume limits.
  • Hardware integrationFasteners, inserts, ports, fans, cables, PCBs, power supplies, displays, and sensors.
  • Service after assemblyAccess, removal, replacement, cleaning, adjustment, and readable assembly intent.
