Do I need a 3D model or a file?+

No. A photo, rough dimensions, a sketch, or a description can be enough to start a conversation. Any paid design work is scoped and agreed separately.

How much will it cost?+

It depends on the design work, size, material, quantity, and finish. A print-ready file and a part that needs designing are different jobs. The detailed builder provides a planning range, not a final quote.

Try the detailed builder
Can you print something I found online?+

Often. Share the file or model link. Printability and the design's license need review before a physical print is confirmed. Buying a design file does not automatically grant every type of commercial use.

Can you recreate a broken part?+

Often, depending on its job and the available measurements. Start with a photo beside a ruler and explain how it is used. Safety-critical, heavily loaded, or unsuitable parts may be declined.

I'm not in Tulsa. Can we still work together?+

Yes, for suitable design and print projects. Tulsa pickup and U.S. shipping are available. Shipping, packing, and timing are confirmed with the work.

What happens when I send an inquiry?+

Your inquiry is reviewed before any work or payment is agreed. If online delivery is unavailable, you can email or download your inquiry. The confirmation explains whether your files were uploaded.
