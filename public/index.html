Before you ask

Common questions

Can you print an STL I found online?

Usually. Send the model link or file and confirm its license allows the intended use. I will review printability, size, material, color, and expected finish before confirming the order.

Can you recreate a broken plastic part?

Often. Photos beside a ruler help, but accurate caliper measurements or the original part are better. Some safety-critical, regulated, or heavily loaded parts may be declined or treated as prototypes.

How accurate is the rough quote?

It is a planning range, not a checkout total. It becomes much tighter when you provide slicer grams and machine time. Design and repair work needs human review because unknowns matter.

Do you ship?

Yes, within the United States for suitable work. Tulsa pickup or local handoff is also available. Shipping, taxes, unusual packing, and replacement parts are confirmed separately.

What will you not make?

I decline work that is illegal, unsafe, deceptive, beyond the capabilities of the process, or not properly licensed. See the project terms for the practical boundaries.
