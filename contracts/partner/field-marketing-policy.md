# PROPOSED Permitted Outreach and Safety Policy
**DRAFT FOR ATTORNEY REVIEW / NOT EFFECTIVE**

## [POL-01] Respectful interactions
Introduce yourself factually as an independent marketing partner describing services of {{company_dba}}, where lawful. Do not claim authority to bind contracts or collect payments. Never mention personal hardship, personal circumstances, or prior assistance as a sales argument.

## [POL-02] Permission-based distribution
Obtain permission from business management when leaving samples/flyers. Follow no-soliciting/no-trespassing signs and leave immediately if requested. Do not place literature in USPS mailboxes. Residential door placement and street/sidewalk solicitation require counsel-confirmed municipal rules and campaign permission, not an automatic default.

## [POL-03] Safety
Use sound judgment around traffic, weather, dangerous locations and interactions. No requirement to enter unsafe premises. Helmet/lights/lock education offered without making equipment investment a job precondition. Emergency situations prioritize safety over deliverable completion.

## [POL-04] Data privacy
Collect no private phone/email/name unless person agrees specifically to be contacted about {{company_dba}} services. Never photograph individuals, capture card details, access phone contacts, or use background GPS. Respect unsubscribe/withdrawal requests and inform client promptly.

## [POL-05] Product claims
Use only current approved sample descriptions and generalized services. Prices and turnaround require owner approval; no guarantee of payment acceptance, printer performance or deadlines. Promotional materials remain client intellectual property.

## [POL-06] Autonomy
Specific result/quality/safety standards apply to accepted gig, but this draft does not prescribe shifts, routes, constant reporting or apparel. Contractor status requires independent professional review of real practices.
