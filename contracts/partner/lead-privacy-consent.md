# PROPOSED Business Contact Follow-Up Consent Language
**DRAFT FOR LEGAL/PRIVACY REVIEW**

## [CONSENT-01] Plain-language script
"I help introduce local businesses to {{company_dba}}, which offers custom 3D printing and design. Would you like the business to contact you about your project? If yes, may I record your preferred name and business contact method for that purpose? It's completely optional."

## [CONSENT-02] Permission record
Capture: company/venue; opt-in yes/no; permitted contact channel; opt-in date/time; statement shown/said and version; agreed purpose; source campaign; optional preferred follow-up time. If 'no', store only business-level nonpersonal visit outcome. Never imply consent from accepting a flyer or QR sample.

## [CONSENT-03] Opt-out
"You can ask us not to contact you again by emailing {{company_contact_email}}. We'll stop promotional follow-up and retain only records required for compliance."

## [CONSENT-04] Marketing actions
No automated SMS, cold email blasts or third-party contact exports by default. If later added, assess CAN-SPAM, TCPA and state laws separately and honor suppression lists. Maintain accurate source provenance and do not expose contacts to other partners.
