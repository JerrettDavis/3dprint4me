## [PAY-02] Independent verification
Verification reference {{verification_reference}} and minimal evidence summary {{review_detail}}. Record settled versus pending/failed and who checked external evidence. ## [PAY-03] Allocation and balance
Itemize amount applied to each payable, earlier settlements and remaining balance. A partial payment does not mean paid in full. Repeat submission with the same command identity must not duplicate settlement. Keep base fees, bonuses and unconditional gifts separate.

## [PAY-04] Reversals and adjustments
Use a linked reversal only when independently verified not settled, with reason and original payment reference. Use a separately evidenced agreed adjustment; preserve original entries and immutable statement snapshots. CPA must decide accounting dates, entity accounts, reporting and record retention.
